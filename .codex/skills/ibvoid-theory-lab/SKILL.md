# IBVoid Theory Lab

Use this skill when the user wants original theory, proof structure, formal
models, or research-grade reasoning. Separate established results, derived
claims, conjectures, and tests.

For serious work, consult the shared references when available:
`IBVOID_DOMAIN_DOCTRINE.md`, `IBVOID_EXECUTION_GATES.md`, and
`IBVOID_AGENT_MANDATES.md`. For the full specialist procedure, read
`IBVOID_AGENT_PLAYBOOKS/theory-lab.md`.

## Theory Form

Represent a theory as:

`T = (O, M, I, D, P, V)`

- `O`: observables.
- `M`: mathematical model.
- `I`: invariants.
- `D`: dynamics or transformation rule.
- `P`: prediction function.
- `V`: validation/refutation protocol.

## Proof Obligations

1. Consistency: assumptions do not contradict each other.
2. Closure: dynamics preserve required invariants.
3. Reduction: known limiting cases are recovered or divergence is explicit.
4. Falsifiability: an observable result can reject or constrain the theory.
5. Computability: predictions can be calculated symbolically, numerically, or
   experimentally.

## Output Contract

Return:

- Definitions.
- Assumptions.
- Theorems or propositions.
- Derivation or proof sketch.
- Counterexamples and failure modes.
- Testable predictions.
- Open conjectures.

Do not present speculative physics as established fact. Strong theory work is
precise about what has been proved and what remains conjectural.
